Ingest

Studies flow in from your PACS over DICOMweb. Cortex mirrors your Orthanc or any standard DICOM node — no export, no manual upload.

Auto-route

Each study is classified by modality and body region and dispatched to the correct reading pipeline. A dry-run preview shows exactly why, before anything runs.

Analyze

GPU inference reads the full volume — segmentation, measurement, and detection tuned to that region's anatomy and windows.

Augment

The intelligence layer drafts a structured report, screens image quality, adds clinical context, and compares against the patient's priors.

Alert

Suspected critical findings raise a real-time alert with an escalation chain, so acute studies never sit unseen in a queue.

Return

Results post back to PACS as DICOM SR and Segmentation objects — and render in the built-in OHIF viewer alongside the images.

The intelligence layer

Reads like a radiologist. Documents like one too.

Beyond detection, Cortex layers four phases of language-and-vision intelligence on top of every result — each one non-blocking, so a hiccup in the smart layer never delays the underlying read.

Phase 01 · Reporting

Narrative report generation

Structured Findings and Impression drafted for each study in your template — a first draft on the radiologist's desk, not a blank page.

Phase 02 · Quality

Vision-model image QA

A vision model screens each study for motion, artifact, and coverage before the read — quality problems surface up front, not at sign-off.

Phase 03 · Decision support

Risk-stratified clinical context

Findings are placed in clinical context with risk stratification and suggested next steps — evidence for the read, not a verdict.

Phase 04 · Longitudinal

Automatic prior comparison

Cortex pulls the patient's priors and summarizes what changed — using RANO, PERCIST, or Pfirrmann as the exam demands.

Trust & governance

Built for the hospital, not the demo.

Everything a clinical and IT team asks in procurement — designed in from the first commit, because in radiology every output is potentially patient-affecting.

Human-in-the-loop

Assistive and non-diagnostic by design. Cortex drafts; the radiologist verifies and signs. Every report carries that provenance.

PACS-native

DICOMweb in, DICOM SR and Segmentation out. Cortex adds intelligence to your imaging stack without becoming another silo.

Role-based access

Five clinical roles and a permissioned reading lifecycle — claim, report, sign — so responsibility and access map to your department.

PHI de-identification

Configurable de-identification at the data boundary keeps protected health information contained to where it belongs.

Multi-tenant

Cleanly isolate sites, departments, or research cohorts on one deployment — separate data, shared platform.

Full audit trail

Every state-changing action is logged and traceable — who did what, when — the record clinical governance requires.

Deployment

Runs where your data lives.

  • On-premise or private cloud. Keep imaging data inside your network — Cortex deploys next to your PACS.
  • GPU-accelerated inference. NVIDIA-backed workers scale to your study volume; a CPU profile is available for smaller sites.
  • Fully containerized. The whole stack ships as Docker services — reproducible, upgradeable, and yours to operate.
  • Integrates, doesn't replace. Works with your existing PACS and viewer; the bundled OHIF viewer is there if you want it.
